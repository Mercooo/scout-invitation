Every scout learns it first

Be prepared.

You can pitch a tent in the rain. Tie a knot with cold hands. Find north without a phone.

But there's one question no badge ever covered —

Does God actually know your name?

A compass doesn't point to where you are.

It points to where you were made to go. It only works because north doesn't move.

God isn't a rule on a list. He's true north — and He wants to walk the trail with you, personally.

He isn't hiding.
He's knocking.

“Behold, I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears my voice and opens the door, then I will come in to him, and will dine with him, and he with me.” Revelation 3:20

No pressure. No performance. Just a door — and it opens from your side.

You don't have to climb to reach Him.

He's already on the trail, coming down to meet you.

He's reaching for you.

Will you take His hand?

“I, the Lord your God, will hold your right hand, saying to you, ‘Don’t be afraid. I will help you.’” Isaiah 41:13

How the night unfolds

  1. Afternoon

    Gather

    Drop your bags, grab a hot drink, meet people. Nobody's performing.

  2. Dusk

    Campfire & worship

    Songs around the fire. Sing loud or just listen — both count.

  3. Night

    The Invitation

    A short, honest talk. No guilt trip. One simple question: will you say yes to Him?

  4. Later

    The silent walk

    Thirty minutes alone on a lantern-lit trail. Just you and God, and nobody watching.

  5. Late

    Open prayer

    Pray out loud, pray silently, or just sit by the embers with a friend.

  6. Sunrise

    First light

    Breakfast as the sun comes up. A fresh start, quite literally.

  • An open heart — weighs nothing
